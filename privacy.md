# Vision Privacy Policy

[Português](privacidade) · **English**

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

Vision is a personal finance app made by an independent developer, Dungeon Dev Labs. This policy explains which data the app uses and why.

## Your financial data stays on your device

Everything you enter is saved **only on your phone**, in a local database:
- chests (your savings);
- deposits and withdrawals;
- fixed expenses and payments;
- spending tracker: accounts, cards, entries, categories, caps and statements you import;
- loans and installments;
- settings.

Vision has **no account, login or server of its own**, and doesn't send this data anywhere.

## Bank notifications (optional)

If you give Vision access to notifications and choose the bank of an account or card, Vision reads **only that bank's notifications** to turn purchases and Pix into entries. Other apps' notifications are ignored right away. The text is read on the device and **sent nowhere**. The ones the app doesn't understand stay in a local list (the last 10) you can clear in Settings. To stop, turn off Vision's access in Android's notification settings.

## Backup

- **File:** you can export a backup file and save it wherever you want. Vision doesn't read or send this file without your action.
- **Automatic backup to Google Drive (optional):** if you tap "Connect Google Drive", Vision asks Google for access **only to the app's own data folder** in your Drive (scope `drive.appdata`). It's hidden and only Vision can see it: the app does **not** access your other files. Once a day the app saves a copy of your data there, and you can restore it on another device. The file stays in **your** Google account; the developer has no access to it. To stop, tap "Disconnect" or remove Vision's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Vision's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## What the app fetches from the internet

Vision uses the internet only to **download** public information, without sending your data:
- **CDI rate:** public series from the Central Bank of Brazil (api.bcb.gov.br), used to calculate the chests' yield.
- **News:** public headlines (RSS) from Valor Econômico and Money Times. Tapping a story opens it in your browser, on the source's site.
- **Mascot lines and dragons:** a public text file on GitHub with the lines and the dragons.
- **Bank notification templates:** a public file on GitHub (banks.json) that says how to read each bank's notifications.
- **Community:** public tips from r/VisionDungeon on Reddit. Tapping to post opens Reddit in your browser or its app, under Reddit's privacy rules.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

Some screens show a Google AdMob ad, and you can choose to watch a video to unlock a dragon.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect device data such as the advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction information, under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in Android settings. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Union, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

## Fingerprint or PIN

If you turn on the lock, Vision uses Android's own biometrics or PIN. The app only receives "unlocked" or "not unlocked"; your fingerprint never reaches the app.

## Notifications

The app sends local notifications with the day's yield, reminders for fixed expenses, installments and card bills, and spending cap alerts. They're generated on the device, with no server.

## Support via Pix

The "Support Vision" area only copies the developer's Pix key. The transfer happens in your bank's app, and Vision has no access to it.

## Children

Vision is not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights

Since your financial data stays only on your device, you have full control over it:
- to delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings;
- the Google Drive backup is removed when you remove Vision's access in your Google account;
- for ad data, see Google's options above.
